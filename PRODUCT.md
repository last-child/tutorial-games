# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

static HTML/CSS/JS — a single self-contained `.html` file that opens by double-clicking, no build step.

## Users

Friends and acquaintances of 후니 who drop by to get to know them. Casual, curious visitors, usually arriving from a shared link.

## Product Purpose

A personal self-introduction page for 후니. Success is a friend leaving knowing who 후니 is and what they enjoy, and having fun clicking around.

## Positioning

A self-introduction dressed up as a game's official homepage. The "game" is a concept only: no real game exists, so the page must not present one as real (no fake release dates, store links, ratings, or download buttons that imply a product).

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Korean-language copy.
- Must include a button that shows a "반가워요!" alert when pressed (requested earlier, kept across versions).
- The root `index.html` is the main self-introduction page with the casual games; the game folders sit beside it. Older side pages live in `etc/`: `hooni-lab.html` (HOONI LAB community-style page), `cyworld.html` (2000s minihompy-style page), `chibi.html` (3D chibi toy-room page, rebuilt 2026-10-06 after an earlier version was rejected). Links between pages are fine.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: 후니.
- Hobbies: 게임, 야구, 여행 — shown as three separate cards (user request).
- The user likes emoji-rich, playful pages.

## Evidence on Hand

- No photos, real game, screenshots, or friend testimonials exist. Diary entries, follower counts, and friend comments on earlier pages were placeholders invented by Claude; do not present invented content as real.
- Favorite things used on earlier pages (피자, 강아지, 밤하늘, 아이스크림, 영화, 무지개, 만화책) were also Claude placeholders, never confirmed by the user.

## Product Principles

- Fun to poke at: every visible element that looks interactive should respond.
- Honest concept: borrow the game-site shape, never fake a real game.
- Opens anywhere: one file, no install, works on phone and desktop.
